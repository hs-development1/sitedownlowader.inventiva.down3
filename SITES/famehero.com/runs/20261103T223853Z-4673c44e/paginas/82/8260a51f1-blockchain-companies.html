Smoothly Transitioning Your Blockchain PR Strategy

  • Vet for Web3 Expertise: Ensure alternatives understand blockchain nuances, not just general tech PR.
  • Scrutinize Contracts: Look for clear deliverables, exit clauses, and data ownership specific to crypto.
  • Define KPIs Early: Establish measurable goals (e.g., media mentions in top crypto outlets, sentiment analysis) before switching.
  • Set Realistic Timelines: Expect a 1-3 month transition, factoring in crypto market volatility.
  • Avoid Pitfalls: Don't compromise on specialized knowledge or overlook contract details. Maintain consistent messaging throughout the handover.

Common Pitfalls When Choosing PR Support for Blockchain Ventures

  • Focusing solely on technology jargon, alienating journalists and the general public instead of crafting accessible narratives.
  • Not understanding the target audience, leading to ineffective messaging and wasted outreach for niche products.
  • Overhyping token launches and making unrealistic promises, risking legal issues and damaging credibility.
  • Neglecting compliance language, including necessary disclaimers and adhering to regulatory language.
  • Employing a weak distribution strategy, publishing on irrelevant sites or at the wrong times for minimal impact.
  • Treating PR as a one-time effort rather than an ongoing process for building lasting awareness and trust.
